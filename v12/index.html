3 · Your app doesn’t have to be open to work

Download LinkedIn PDF

Captions (Instagram and LinkedIn)

Instagram

Your app doesn't have to be open to work.

Widgets put a small, timely fact on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. Live Activities keep a task with a start and an end in view. Buttons can run an action without opening the app (iOS 17), and they stay inactive until the phone is unlocked.

One more rule from Apple: keep the glance private. Show an innocuous summary and reveal the detail inside the app.

Which moments in your app deserve the Lock Screen?

#iOSdevelopment #digitalhealth #healthtech #appdesign

LinkedIn

The best feature in a health app may be the one people see without opening it.

Here is what Apple's own documentation says:
- Widgets show a small amount of timely information at a glance, on the Home Screen (iOS 14) and the Lock Screen (iOS 16).
- Live Activities (iOS 16.1) suit tasks and events with a defined start and end. A Live Activity stays active for up to eight hours, then up to four more on the Lock Screen.
- Buttons and toggles in widgets and Live Activities (iOS 17) run an action without launching the app. On a locked device they stay inactive until the person unlocks.
- Privacy: avoid sensitive information on screens others can see. Show an innocuous summary and let people open the app for details.

Live Activities are for ongoing tasks, not promotions. Apple says so directly.

We design and build iPhone and iPad apps for health products. If you are mapping which moments belong on the Lock Screen, we would be glad to help.

Sources: Apple WidgetKit, ActivityKit and Human Interface Guidelines.

4 · Google measures three speed numbers

Download LinkedIn PDF

Captions (Instagram and LinkedIn)

Instagram

Google measures three speed numbers. Do you know yours?

LCP: the main content appears in 2.5 seconds or less.
INP: the page responds to a tap in 200 ms or less.
CLS: the page doesn't jump around (0.1 or less).

Measure real visits at the 75th percentile, phone and desktop separately. And a fair warning: Google says good scores don't guarantee top rankings.

#webdesign #webperformance #digitalhealth #healthtech

LinkedIn

Google publishes three speed and stability numbers called Core Web Vitals. Do you know yours?

- LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): how fast the main content appears. Good is 2.5 seconds or less.
- INP (Interaction to Next Paint): how fast the page responds to a click, tap or key press. Good is 200 milliseconds or less.
- CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): how much the page jumps around. Good is 0.1 or less.

Measure real page loads at the 75th percentile and report phone and desktop separately.

An honest note on expectations: Google recommends good Core Web Vitals for success with Search, but says there is no single signal, good scores do not guarantee top rankings, and relevant content still comes first.

8 · Notifications people keep on

Download LinkedIn PDF

Captions (Instagram and LinkedIn)

Instagram

Notifications people keep on.

Five rules from Apple's own guidelines:
1. Ask when it makes sense, not at first launch.
2. Start quietly with provisional delivery.
3. Let people act without opening the app.
4. Time Sensitive is a privilege.
5. Keep it private.

#iOSdevelopment #digitalhealth #healthtech #appdesign

LinkedIn

One badly timed notification and people turn them all off. Apple's own documentation gives us five rules worth following.

1. Ask in context. Apple recommends requesting permission when people can see why, not automatically at first launch.
2. Start quietly. Provisional authorization (iOS 12) delivers to Notification Center without a sound or banner, with Keep and Turn Off buttons.
3. Let people act. Actionable notifications respond without launching the app. Prefer time-saving, nondestructive actions, and never an action that only opens the app.
4. Treat Time Sensitive (iOS 15) as a privilege. It can break through Focus and Notification Summary, and people can turn it off.
5. Keep it private. Avoid sensitive, personal or confidential information in a notification.

We design the whole flow, from the first permission to the tap.

Sources: Apple, UserNotifications documentation and Human Interface Guidelines.

10 · Shipped last year doesn’t mean ready this year

Download LinkedIn PDF

Captions (Instagram and LinkedIn)

Instagram

Shipped last year doesn't mean ready this year.

Five checks for a live web and mobile product:
1. Speed: LCP, INP and CLS on real visits.
2. Test on the latest iOS (iOS 27 shipped 14 September 2026).
3. Find where people stop in your key flows.
4. Review notifications and permissions.
5. Can people find the next step?

#digitalhealth #healthtech #webdesign #iOSdevelopment

LinkedIn

A product that shipped last year is not automatically ready for this year. Five checks worth running on a live web and mobile product:

1. Speed. Check the three Core Web Vitals on real visits: LCP 2.5 seconds or less, INP 200 ms or less, CLS 0.1 or less.
2. The latest iOS. iOS 27 shipped on 14 September 2026. Open the app on it and check widgets, Live Activities, permissions and notification flows.
3. Key flows. Follow sign-up, intake or booking end to end and fix the step where most people stop.
4. Notifications. Is the ask in context, is anything repeated, is the text private on a Lock Screen?
5. Navigation. Can a visitor find the next step on every page?

If you want a second pair of eyes on your product, we would be glad to take a look.

Sources: web.dev, Web Vitals; Apple Newsroom; Apple Human Interface Guidelines.
